Protecting your finances online starts with a few simple habits: use strong, unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA), monitor your accounts regularly, and think twice before sharing personal information. This guide explains the most common financial scams, how cybercriminals target different age groups, and practical ways to protect children, teens, older adults, and yourself from fraud and identity theft.
