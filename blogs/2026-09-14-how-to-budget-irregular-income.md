The key to budgeting irregular income is to identify your bare-bones spending needs, then make a plan for any income you earn above that. It’s important to account for taxes, savings, and discretionary spending on top of your basic living expenses. Set yourself up for financial success with clear priorities on how to handle both busy and slow months.
